# CUSTOMER SUPPORT TERMS AND CONDITIONS
## AI Loyalty Platform

### 1. Support Responsibilities
- Support users are responsible for handling customer queries related to the loyalty platform.
- Support users must provide accurate information based on approved policies.
- Support users must maintain professional communication with customers.

### 2. Customer Query Management
- Support users can investigate questions about reward points, transactions and redemption.
- Customers may request assistance with missing points, failed redemptions and account access issues.
- Each complaint should be recorded and tracked through the approved support process.

### 3. Reward Point Disputes
- Support users must verify transaction details before responding to point-related complaints.
- Invoice details and transaction references may be requested for verification.
- Support users must not manually change reward point balances unless explicitly authorized through an approved process.

### 4. Redemption Support
- Support users can assist customers with voucher, cashback and merchandise redemption queries.
- Redemption status must be checked before providing confirmation.
- Failed redemption requests should be escalated to the relevant fulfillment team.

### 5. Account Assistance
- Support users can assist customers with login and account-related issues.
- Identity verification must be completed before sharing sensitive account information.
- Passwords and OTPs must never be requested from customers.

### 6. Data Privacy
- Customer personal information must remain confidential.
- Support users must access customer records only when necessary to resolve authorized queries.
- Customer information must not be downloaded or shared without permission.

### 7. Escalation Process
- Technical issues must be escalated to the technical team.
- Reward calculation disputes must be escalated to the loyalty operations team.
- Suspected fraud must be escalated to the administrator.
- Unresolved customer complaints must follow the approved escalation process.

### 8. Acknowledgement
By using the platform, support users agree to follow these sample terms and applicable organizational policies.
