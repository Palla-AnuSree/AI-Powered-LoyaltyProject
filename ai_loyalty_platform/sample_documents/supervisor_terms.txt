# SUPERVISOR TERMS AND CONDITIONS
## AI Loyalty Platform

### 1. Role and Responsibilities
- Supervisors are responsible for monitoring assigned outlet users and operational activities.
- Supervisors must ensure that outlet users follow approved loyalty program procedures.
- Supervisors must use their assigned accounts for all platform activities.

### 2. Team Management
- Supervisors can monitor the performance of authorized outlet users.
- Supervisors can review assigned outlet sales and achievement reports.
- Supervisors must escalate unusual activities or policy violations.
- Supervisors cannot assign administrator privileges.

### 3. Sales Verification
- Supervisors must review suspicious or disputed sales transactions.
- Invoice information must be verified before recommending corrections.
- Duplicate or invalid sales entries must be escalated.
- Supervisors must not approve transactions that violate loyalty program rules.

### 4. Target and Achievement Monitoring
- Supervisors can monitor sales targets and achievements for assigned outlets.
- Target calculations follow the active campaign configuration.
- Supervisors must report discrepancies in target calculations.
- Changes to campaign rules require authorized administrative approval.

### 5. Reward Management
- Supervisors can monitor reward point transactions and redemption activities within their permitted access.
- Supervisors cannot independently manipulate customer point balances.
- Suspicious reward transactions must be escalated to the administration team.

### 6. Data Privacy
- Supervisors must protect customer and outlet information.
- Reports must only be accessed for authorized business purposes.
- Confidential information must not be shared outside approved channels.

### 7. Escalation
- Suspected fraudulent activity must be reported to the administrator.
- Customer disputes that cannot be resolved at outlet level must be escalated to support.
- Supervisors must maintain accurate records of escalated issues.

### 8. Acknowledgement
By accessing the platform, supervisors agree to follow these sample terms and organizational policies.
