# OUTLET USER TERMS AND CONDITIONS
## AI Loyalty Platform

### 1. Account Responsibilities
- Outlet users can access the platform only through their assigned accounts.
- Each outlet user is associated with an authorized outlet.
- Outlet users must not share their login credentials.
- Outlet users must not access information belonging to unauthorized outlets.

### 2. Sales Entry
- Outlet users are responsible for entering accurate sales information.
- Sales entries must contain valid invoice numbers, purchase dates, brand information and eligible sales amounts.
- Duplicate invoice submissions are prohibited.
- Incorrect sales information must be reported for correction.

### 3. Reward Point Processing
- Reward points are calculated according to the configured loyalty program rules.
- Outlet users cannot manually modify customer reward point balances.
- Only eligible and verified transactions can generate reward points.
- Transactions rejected by the system must be reviewed through the authorized process.

### 4. Customer Information
- Outlet users must collect and process customer information only for authorized business purposes.
- Customer mobile numbers and personal details must remain confidential.
- Customer information must not be shared with unauthorized parties.

### 5. Reward Redemption
- Outlet users may assist customers with eligible reward redemption processes.
- Redemption must follow configured reward eligibility and availability rules.
- Outlet users must not process unauthorized or duplicate redemption requests.

### 6. Outlet Performance
- Outlet users can view reports and dashboards permitted for their assigned outlet.
- Sales and target information must be accurate.
- Any discrepancy in outlet performance reports must be reported to the supervisor.

### 7. Prohibited Activities
- Manipulating sales entries to generate additional reward points is prohibited.
- Creating fraudulent invoices is prohibited.
- Accessing another outlet's records without authorization is prohibited.

### 8. Acknowledgement
By using the platform, outlet users agree to follow these sample terms and the applicable outlet operating policies.
