# ADMIN TERMS AND CONDITIONS
## AI Loyalty Platform

### 1. Purpose
The Admin is responsible for managing and maintaining the loyalty platform, including users, outlets, campaigns, rewards and platform configurations.

### 2. User Management
- Admins can create customer, outlet user, supervisor and support accounts.
- Admins are responsible for assigning appropriate roles and access permissions.
- Admins must deactivate accounts that are no longer authorized to use the platform.
- Admins must not share temporary passwords or login credentials through unsecured channels.

### 3. Loyalty Program Management
- Admins can configure loyalty campaigns and reward programs.
- Admins can define reward point rules, eligibility criteria and redemption limits.
- Any modification to reward calculations must be reviewed before activation.
- Changes to active campaigns must be communicated to affected users.

### 4. Reward Configuration
- Admins can configure reward categories, voucher values and redemption limits.
- Admins must ensure that reward configurations follow approved business policies.
- Duplicate or fraudulent reward configurations are prohibited.

### 5. Data Privacy and Security
- Admins must protect customer information, including mobile numbers and personal identification details.
- Customer information must not be disclosed to unauthorized individuals.
- Admin access credentials must remain confidential.
- Admins must report suspected data breaches immediately.

### 6. Reporting and Monitoring
- Admins can access platform-wide reports and dashboards.
- Admins are responsible for monitoring unusual transactions and system activity.
- Reports containing customer information must be handled securely.

### 7. Policy Violations
Unauthorized access, data misuse or manipulation of reward transactions may result in account suspension and further investigation.

### 8. Acknowledgement
By accessing the platform, the Admin agrees to follow these sample terms and the applicable organizational policies.
