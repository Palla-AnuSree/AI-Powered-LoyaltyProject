# CUSTOMER TERMS AND CONDITIONS
## AI Loyalty Platform

### 1. Program Eligibility
- Customers must have an active registered loyalty account.
- Each customer must use their registered mobile number to access the platform.
- Customer accounts are personal and cannot be transferred to another individual.

### 2. Reward Point Earning
- Customers earn 10 reward points for every ₹100 spent on eligible purchases, subject to campaign rules.
- Points are calculated based on approved sales transactions.
- Points are credited only after successful transaction confirmation.
- Cancelled, rejected or invalid transactions are not eligible for reward points.

### 3. Reward Point Redemption
- Customers can redeem accumulated points for available rewards.
- Redemption is subject to the customer's available point balance.
- Some rewards may have minimum point requirements.
- Once a redemption is successfully completed, the redeemed points are deducted from the customer's account.

### 4. Reward Point Expiry
- Reward points may have an expiry date depending on the applicable campaign.
- Expired points cannot be redeemed.
- Customers should review their point balance and expiry information regularly.

### 5. Point Transfer
- Reward points cannot be transferred between customer accounts.
- Customers cannot sell, exchange or transfer reward points to another person.

### 6. Account Security
- Customers are responsible for protecting their login credentials.
- Customers must not share their passwords or OTPs.
- Suspected unauthorized account activity should be reported to customer support.

### 7. Transaction Disputes
- Customers can contact customer support if eligible purchase points are missing.
- Customers may be required to provide invoice or transaction details.
- All disputes are subject to transaction verification.

### 8. Fraudulent Activities
- Artificial transactions, duplicate invoice submissions and misuse of promotional offers are prohibited.
- Accounts involved in suspected fraudulent activity may be temporarily suspended pending investigation.

### 9. Acknowledgement
By participating in the loyalty program, customers agree to follow these sample terms and applicable campaign conditions.
